When TSPITR is used

Logical corruption limited to:

One or more tablespaces

You do not want to impact the whole database

Requires:

ARCHIVELOG mode

SYSTEM, SYSAUX, UNDO cannot be recovered with TSPITR

Key architectural concept (do not skip)

TSPITR uses an auxiliary database internally.

Oracle:

Restores the tablespace to a past time

Exports metadata

Imports it back into the target DB

That’s why you need:

Auxiliary destination

Extra disk space

TSPITR workflow (mental model)

Identify corrupted tablespace

Identify recovery time/SCN

RMAN creates auxiliary instance

Tablespace is recovered in isolation

Metadata is reattached to target DB

Target database stays OPEN.
